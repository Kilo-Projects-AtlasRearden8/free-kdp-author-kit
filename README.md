# Free KDP Author Kit

A free, beginner-friendly guide and directory of **official** free Kindle Direct Publishing (KDP) resources for authors: cover templates, blurb (book description) help, category pickers, and royalty calculators.

Built and maintained by **Asdale Dynamics, LLC** — beginner-friendly AI education, books, and practical tools for authors and small businesses.

## Who this is for

Dylan Asdale is a registered nurse turned AI author and founder of Asdale Dynamics, LLC (Indiana, formed October 2026). His book *The Massive AI Manual* and hub site dylanasdale.github.io explain AI "like a friend would": plain English, step by step, never condescending.

This kit is for authors self-publishing on Amazon KDP who want free, official tools — no sign-ups, no paid upgrades, no affiliate links.

## What's inside

| Category | What it is |
|---|---|
| Cover templates | Free official KDP paperback and eBook cover templates and layout guidance |
| Blurb writers | Official KDP guidance for writing your book description (blurb) |
| Category pickers | Official Amazon Kindle Store categories and best-seller browsing |
| Royalty calculators | Official KDP royalty information and calculation guidance |

## How to use

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. No build step, no internet required after the page loads (links open official Amazon/KDP pages). Use the search box to filter resources by name, category, or tag.

## Official links only

Every link in this directory points to an official Amazon / KDP domain (`amazon.com` or `kdp.amazon.com`). No third-party directories, no affiliate links, no paid tools.
